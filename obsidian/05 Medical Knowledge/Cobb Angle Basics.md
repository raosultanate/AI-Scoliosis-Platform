# Cobb angle basics

The Cobb angle is constructed from selected upper and lower end vertebral endplates. Version 1A
represents each endplate as a line segment between clinician-provided left and right corner points,
then computes the acute angle between the undirected lines.

> [!warning]
> This note is preliminary engineering context, not clinical guidance. It has not yet been reviewed
> by a qualified clinician or grounded in a summarized source paper.

Important sources of variability include landmark placement, end-vertebra selection, image
orientation, measurement convention, and whether multiple curves are present. These limit direct
comparison to a dataset scalar until its protocol is known. See [[ADR-002 Cobb Selection Policy]].
