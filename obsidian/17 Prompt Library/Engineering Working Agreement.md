# Engineering working agreement

Treat the repository as a long-lived, clinically consequential product. Prefer readable, modular,
typed, documented, deterministic, and testable code. Keep business logic out of visualization and
external-format details out of geometry. Never jump beyond the active roadmap version.

For Version 1A: use only clinician-provided landmarks. Do not add ML, neural networks, training,
PyTorch, inference, an API, a frontend, or a database. Read the vault before work; update the daily
journal, ADRs, TODOs, and relevant knowledge notes before handoff.
