# Product vision

Build a clinically meaningful AI rehabilitation platform that begins with auditable scoliosis
geometry and can later expand into landmark detection, curve classification, skeletal maturity,
posture, goniometry, exercise recognition, coaching, progress tracking, clinician workflows, and
patient-facing tools.

The sequencing constraint is central: validate data contracts and geometry before introducing ML.
See [[Version 1A Roadmap]].
